GeoGuessr

Explore the world and guess locations!

How to Play

  1. You'll be shown a random location in Google Street View
  2. Explore the area by navigating around and looking at your surroundings
  3. Use the compass at the top to see which direction you're facing
  4. When you think you know where you are, click on the world map to place your guess
  5. Click "Make Guess" to submit your answer
  6. You'll earn points based on how close your guess is to the actual location
  7. Play 5 rounds and try to get the highest score possible!

Scoring:

  • Perfect guess (< 1km): 5000 points
  • Very close (< 10km): 4000+ points
  • Close (< 100km): 3000+ points
  • Far (< 1000km): 1000+ points
  • Very far (> 5000km): 0 points
